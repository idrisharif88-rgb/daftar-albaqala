Delete your account — ShopBook

ShopBook · com.shopbookidris.app

You can delete your account and all your data in either of two ways.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate, permanent, and cannot be undone.

Option 2: by email

Write to idrisharif88@gmail.com and include the phone number registered in the app.

We action the request within 30 days of receiving it.

What is deleted

  • The account itself: phone number, shop name, and the hashed password.
  • All recorded contacts: names, phone numbers, and notes.
  • All entries: debts, payments, amounts, and dates.
  • Price lists, items, and saved baskets.
  • Account settings and exchange rates.

Deletion is complete and permanent — no part of this data is retained afterwards.

Data stored on your phone

Deleting from inside the app also wipes the local copy. If you delete your account by email instead, remove the copy on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.
